Lake Martin specialists

DOCK POWER THAT'S SAFE TO SWIM NEAR

A dock is the most demanding circuit at a lake house. It sits on Alabama Power shoreline, it stays wet, and a fault can put current in the water. We build and inspect dock systems with that in mind.

  • GFCI-protected dock subpanels in NEMA 3R enclosures, sized for lifts, lights and shore power.
  • Boat lift and PWC lift circuits, including motor replacement wiring and remote controls.
  • Spring dock safety inspection with a leakage-current test in the water around your dock.
  • Away-owner service: we text photo reports when you're in Birmingham, Montgomery or Atlanta.
Building a new dock? Alabama Power shoreline permits take 4 to 8 weeks. Request an estimate when you apply so the electrical plan is ready when the permit clears.
